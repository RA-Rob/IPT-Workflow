# POC vs. Funded Delivery Separation

The IPT Workflow treats POC construction as *input* to the workflow, not as a phase within it. Gate 0 assumes a working POC and a committed funding customer already exist. The question of how POC work is funded, staffed, and time-boxed belongs to a separate ideation-to-product model not yet documented.

## Key Points
- **POC is input, not a phase** — the workflow begins at Gate 0, which assumes the POC exists and a funding customer has already committed.
- The old "shop or shelve" gate and the shopping interlude were removed entirely; vendor shopping now appears only as contextual background, not as a gated step.
- **The gap is flagged as the top open question** across docs/02, 04, index.md, and the Executive Summary: how does POC funding, staffing, and time-boxing work?
- The broader "ideation-to-product" concept — covering how speculative self-funded exploration (the POC stage) is managed — is not yet documented anywhere in the framework.
- Putting speculative self-funded exploration under the same charter-and-gate machinery built for funded delivery is the wrong trade; the two models are structurally different and should remain separate.

## Details

The removal of POC construction from the phase structure reflects a fundamental scoping decision: the IPT Workflow's gates and phases are designed for funded, committed delivery — a team that has a customer, a budget, and a working prototype. Applying that machinery to the earlier, speculative phase (where you're still building a POC to find a customer or justify funding) creates a mismatch. The rigor appropriate for funded delivery is bureaucratic overhead during exploration.

The practical consequence is a documented gap: the framework currently has no answer for "how does the POC get built and funded?" Leadership ask #1 in the Executive Summary surfaces this directly. The intent is that a separate ideation-to-product document would cover the pre-Gate-0 world — POC scoping, self-funding constraints, time-boxing heuristics — while the IPT Workflow document covers everything from Gate 0 onward.
