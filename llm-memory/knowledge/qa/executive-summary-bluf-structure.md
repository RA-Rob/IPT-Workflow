# Q: What belongs in the IPT Workflow Executive Summary?

## Answer

The Executive Summary uses a BLUF (Bottom Line Up Front) structure: a one-sentence headline followed by 7 bullets summarizing the framework's key claims, then 4 leadership asks in a numbered list.

**The 7 BLUF bullets cover:**
1. The two-stage/two-wallet model (speculative self-funded exploration vs. funded delivery)
2. Licensed core — the reusable framework at the center of each engagement
3. Compound value — how the licensed core improves across engagements
4. Stage-based risk retirement — risk is retired incrementally, not front-loaded
5. 5-phase structure with Gate 2 as the customer's call (proceed to full Build or not)
6. Continuous security — security is woven throughout, not a late-stage gate
7. Durable team model — the team structure persists across engagements

**The 4 leadership asks (numbered list):**
1. How does POC funding, staffing, and time-boxing work? (top open question — not yet answered by the framework)
2–4. (Additional asks covering the framework's dependencies on leadership decisions)

The BLUF block appears at the top of the Executive Summary, before any section headers, so that a reader who stops after the first screen has the complete picture.

See [[concepts/ipt-workflow-phase-gate-structure]] for the phase/gate structure referenced in bullet 5, and [[concepts/poc-vs-funded-delivery-separation]] for the two-stage/two-wallet model referenced in bullet 1.
