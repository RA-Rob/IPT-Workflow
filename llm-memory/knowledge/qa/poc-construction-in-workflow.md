# Q: Should POC construction be part of the IPT Workflow?

## Answer

No. The IPT Workflow treats POC as *input*, not as a phase. Gate 0 — the workflow's entry point — assumes a working POC and a committed funding customer already exist. POC construction, funding, and staffing belong to a separate, not-yet-documented ideation-to-product model.

The old "shop or shelve" gate and the shopping interlude were removed from the framework entirely. Vendor shopping now appears only as context. The reasoning: putting speculative self-funded exploration (building a POC) under the same charter-and-gate machinery designed for funded, committed delivery is structurally wrong. The two models have different funding sources, different risk profiles, and different decision criteria.

The gap is acknowledged and flagged as an open question in docs/02, docs/04, index.md, and the Executive Summary. Leadership ask #1 in the Executive Summary directly surfaces the question: "how does POC funding, staffing, and time-boxing work?"

See [[concepts/poc-vs-funded-delivery-separation]] for the full framing and [[concepts/ipt-workflow-phase-gate-structure]] for Gate 0's assumptions.
