### Session (08:03)

**Context:** Restructuring a multi-doc product development process framework (docs/01–06, templates, README, index.md, nav.yml) to define a POC → Product workflow for an IPT (Integrated Product Team).

**Key Exchanges:**

- Q: How should POC and MVP phases be structured, and should Stand-up be its own phase?
  A: Started at 7 phases/6 gates, then collapsed Stand-up into Gate 1, landing on **5 phases, 5 gates** with the rule "Gate N closes Phase N." Gate 0 = commit and charter; Phases 1–5 = Discovery, MVP, Build, Launch, Operate.

- Q: Should POC construction be part of this workflow?
  A: No — POC is treated as *input*, not a phase. Gate 0 assumes a working POC and a committed funding customer already exist. POC funding/staffing belongs to a broader ideation-to-product concept not yet documented. The gap is flagged as the top open question across docs/02, 04, index.md, and the Executive Summary.

- Q: What belongs in the Executive Summary?
  A: A BLUF section was added: one-sentence headline + 7 bullets covering the two-stage/two-wallet model, licensed core, compound value, stage-based risk retirement, 5-phase structure with Gate 2 as the customer's call, continuous security, and durable team model. Four leadership asks follow in a numbered list.

**Decisions Made:**

- **5 phases, 5 gates** (not 6 or 7): Gate 0 = Commit & Charter; Gate N closes Phase N.
- **Stand-up collapsed into Gate 0** — chartering the team and committing funding are the same act; splitting them added ceremony without adding a decision point.
- **POC construction is explicitly out of scope** — the old "shop or shelve" gate and shopping interlude were removed entirely; shopping now appears only as context.
- **MVP cycles set at 2–3 weeks**; Phase 3 Build length left open.
- **~8 months from customer funding commitment to proven launch**, with POC and shopping outside that count.
- 12 files updated and pushed to `origin/main` (`3635d71`); GitHub Pages rebuild triggered.

**Lessons Learned:**

- Gate/phase numbering shifts cascade widely — docs/01, 02, 03, 05, both templates, README, index.md, and nav.yml all needed touches when the number scheme changed.
- Putting speculative self-funded exploration (POC) under charter-and-gate machinery built for funded delivery is the wrong trade — keep the two models separate.
- Gate 1 (green-light the MVP) is worth keeping distinct from Gate 0 even if both are internal, because it's where the core/tailoring boundary gets committed to paper — an explicit stop that's worth preserving until the team has run it once.
- Six gates plus per-cycle evaluations is too much ceremony for a 4–7 person team; collapsing was the right call.
